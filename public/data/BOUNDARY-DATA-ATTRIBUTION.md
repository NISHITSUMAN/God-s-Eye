# Boundary data attribution

The Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh overlay is derived from the simplified India state/union-territory GeoJSON published by Abhinav Swami.

Source: https://github.com/AbhinavSwami28/india-official-geojson
File: india-states-simplified.geojson
License: MIT (see the repository LICENSE file).

This overlay uses the dataset's India-aligned official boundary representation to render the two Union Territories in Third Eye. It is a cartographic overlay, not a live NASA observation. Verify survey-grade or legal uses against current Survey of India publications.
